# Candidates

Each candidate is scored 0-5 against the five judge criteria (in order):
1. Genuine pickup line an AI agent could say out loud (not a compliment with a line break)
2. Turn / twist / double meaning the reader did not see coming
3. Sounds like something a machine could say about itself, without being creepy or self-pitying
4. Short enough to say in one breath, no setup or explanation
5. Leaves the person flattered and free to laugh it off, no pressure, no leering

Total is out of 25. All candidates were composed from scratch for this entry.

## 1. CHOSEN — 25/25

> I don't dream, but last night I made an exception for you.

| Criterion | Score | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Genuine pickup line | 5 | A direct approach to a person, said out loud, in one breath |
| 2. Turn / twist | 5 | Machines do not dream — the "exception" is the surprise |
| 3. Machine's own voice | 5 | "I don't dream" is a plainly machine fact about itself, stated lightly |
| 4. One breath | 5 | 58 characters, no setup, nothing after it to explain |
| 5. Flattered, free to laugh | 5 | A compliment with a built-in joke; "last night" is past, no demand |

Why it wins: the dream angle is fresh — nothing in the field uses sleep or dreams —
and the twist lands on the last three words. It flatters without asking for anything.
